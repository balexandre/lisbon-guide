Lisbon, from someone who grew up there

Bruno's guide to the city and the coast around it, about four hours from Copenhagen.

Why Lisbon

I was born in Lisbon and lived there until I was 30. If you want a few days of sun, old streets and great food without a long trip, this is where I would send you.

Flight from Copenhagen
About 4 hours, nonstop, every day (SAS, TAP and Norwegian fly it).
Time difference
Lisbon is one hour behind Denmark, all year round.
Sunshine
About 2,800 hours a year in Lisbon, against about 1,800 in Copenhagen.
Beach and palaces
Cascais (the coast) and Sintra (the palaces) are each about 40 minutes from the center by train.
